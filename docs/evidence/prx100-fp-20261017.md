## Correctness and numerical scope

Operands are BF16, with FP32 RNE multiplication followed by sequential FP32 RNE addition in increasing reduction-index order. This is non-fused. Gradual underflow, signed zero, infinity and canonical quiet NaNs are supported; exception flags, NaN payload preservation and other rounding modes are outside the interface.

The directed board cases produce0.375 from fractional operands, exact cancellation to+0, the subnormal2^-127, positive infinity, canonical NaN for0×infinity, +0 after adding a negative-zero product to the initial+0, and overflow to infinity. All48 K1024 runs match CPU reference bits and report4096 cycles.

The pinned `Qwen/Qwen3-0.6B@c1899de289a04d12100db370d81485cdf75e47ca` fixture is the same layer0 q_proj block, channels0..7 and normalized embeddings42/100/1000, SHA256 `409ca5210ca8a10a2799f66590ab0ccee51f1cc501d8dc738a1334a71dfe225e`. Original values are rounded to FP32 then BF16 on the host. All24 outputs are checked by native CPU Float arithmetic and independently reproduced in Linux moonrun/Wasm. CPU equality uses the BF16 operands and sequential FP32 reduction; it does not mean exact equality to the original FP64 calculation or a different reduction order.

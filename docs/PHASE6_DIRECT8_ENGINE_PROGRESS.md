## Conclusions to establish

- Loader and Store hold valid and the complete request payload while waiting
  for local acceptance. Local acceptance moves the engine to Wait; its routed
  ACK advances the real controller. This supplies the persistence qualification
  missing from the arbiter component.
- Acceptance resets the full UInt13 cursors. Committed loader bytes and Store
  ACKs update the row/column recurrence without masking high bits. Configuration
  stays stable during a command, and actual active cursors remain inside their
  admitted shape. Loader Commit and Store indexed-read readiness must be
  conclusions on the composed device, not assumed conditions.
- Core Clear/Run steps advance the real accepted-step counter toward the held
  total. Capture traverses the actual active rectangle, including the first
  capture on core Done, and the final write leaves capture ownership.
- Legal compute acceptance acquires a lease; the actual final capture or
  terminal-without-ownership event releases it. Preserve acceptance priority
  and the original fault path. A local release equation alone is not a bound
  on the time until that release.
